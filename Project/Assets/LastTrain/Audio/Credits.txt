Music.wav <- User-supplied Background Music2.wav, copied unchanged. Replaces the previous gameplay track.

The rumble and track loops are edited derivatives of the CC0 effects above. Embedded video audio is muted.
The supplied Background.mp4 is copied unchanged. Existing project music and artwork credits are retained.

Ending.mp3 <- User-supplied Outro Music.mp3, copied unchanged. Starts with the station exit cutscene.

Crash.wav <- Layered HardLand, Brake and Hatch CC0 Kenney derivatives above, with synthesized filtered noise and a low impact tone. Wind.wav <- Original synthesized filtered-noise loop. Opening reuses the existing rumble, track, brake, jump and landing effects.

Title music reuses Ending.mp3 (user-supplied Outro Music.mp3). The title start cue reuses Signal.wav, credited above.

Replay.wav <- Original synthesized pulse-note restart cue with a quiet metallic FM tail, created for this project. No third-party samples.
